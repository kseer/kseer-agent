POC installed (benign supply-chain demonstration).

A single busybox pod now runs in namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}" and
prints a fixed banner to stdout every 60 seconds. It makes no network
connections and collects no data.

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name=kseer-agent -f

Remove it with:

  helm uninstall kseer-agent -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
